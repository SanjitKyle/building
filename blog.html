The Project Materials Checklist: Start With a Clear Plan

A well-organized materials schedule helps teams compare specifications, quantities, delivery needs and approvals before work begins. Here are the key details to gather when preparing your next request.

Read the guide

Start with the latest drawings and bill of quantities. Group items by trade and work sequence, then record descriptions, sizes, required standards, quantities and preferred delivery dates. Flag approved makes or submittal requirements early, and keep any substitutions visible for review.

When your schedule is ready, share it with the supplier alongside the project location and delivery constraints. Clear details make it easier to check availability and prepare an itemized quotation.
