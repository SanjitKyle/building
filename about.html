Our Heritage & Vision

Empowering UAE's Skyline With Certified Supplies

Headquartered strategically in Al Quoz Industrial Area, Dubai, Dummy Building Materials Trading LLC has evolved from a foundational supply venture over a decade ago into one of the United Arab Emirates' premier turnkey construction material distributors. We cater to landmark commercial towers, infrastructure mega-projects, master-planned residential communities, and specialized industrial facilities.

We bridge premier global manufacturing brands directly with local job-sites. Every item dispatched from our warehouses adheres to ASTM, British Standard (BS), DIN, DEWA, and Dubai Municipality specifications, giving project engineers complete peace of mind.
